# AWS IAM Identity Center

Identity Center is the workforce-access choice for centrally managed access to multiple AWS accounts and applications. Permission sets provision account roles; assignments join a principal, account and permission set. Use external identity-provider federation where appropriate and avoid routine IAM-user credentials for people.

**Exam distinction:** Identity Center serves workforce identities; [[Amazon Cognito]] serves application users.
